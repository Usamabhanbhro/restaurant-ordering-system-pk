## Customer Terms & Conditions

1. Orders are for same-day pickup at the pickup time you choose, within the venue's ordering hours.
2. You pay the venue directly using the payment details shown. The venue's staff check that the payment arrived before your order is accepted.
3. Prices are set by the venue. Any tax is the venue's responsibility.
4. You can cancel until the kitchen start time shown on your order. If your payment was already confirmed, the venue will refund you manually.
5. Your order is held for 30 minutes after the pickup time. After that it is marked a no-show and **is not refunded**.
6. If your payment cannot be found, your order is not accepted. Repeated unconfirmed payments can lead to the venue blocking you from ordering.
7. If the venue cannot confirm your payment in time, the order is cancelled. If you did pay, contact the cafe.
8. Problems with food or refunds are handled by the venue.
